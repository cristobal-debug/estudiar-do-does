Gramática del A2

Past Simple
Acciones terminadas en el pasado. -ed en regulares, formas propias en irregulares, did en negativas y preguntas.I didn't went. → I didn't go.
Present Continuous
Lo que pasa ahora mismo, situaciones temporales y planes ya organizados.Now I work from home this week. → I'm working from home this week.
Future with going to
Planes e intenciones, y predicciones basadas en lo que vemos.I going to travel. → I'm going to travel.
Comparative adjectives
Bigger than, more expensive than, better than… y as… as.More better. → Better.
Superlatives
The biggest, the most beautiful, the best… in the world.The best of the world. → The best in the world.
Countable & uncountable
Some / any, much / many, a lot of, how much / how many.Informations / an advice. → Information / a piece of advice.
Modal verbs: should, must, have to
Consejos (should), obligación (must / have to), falta de obligación (don't have to).You must to study. → You must study.
