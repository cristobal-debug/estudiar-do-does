Unidades

🎓 Unidad 1: Academic English

  • ✓ Write cautious, objective academic prose
  • ✓ Use hedging and nominalisation
  • ✓ Link ideas with advanced connectors

Vocabulario: analyse (analizar), framework (marco (teórico)), thesis (tesis), methodology (metodología), arguably (posiblemente, se podría decir que), plausible (verosímil, plausible), underlying (subyacente), undermine (socavar, debilitar), consistent with (coherente con), peer review (revisión por pares), cite (citar), nevertheless (sin embargo, no obstante).

Lectura: Bilingualism and the brain · Writing: Write an academic paragraph discussing whether technology improves education (180–250 words). Hedge your claims.

🤝 Unidad 2: Professional Communication

  • ✓ Adapt register to audience and purpose
  • ✓ Give diplomatic feedback and negotiate
  • ✓ Write persuasive professional messages

Vocabulario: liaise (coordinarse, hacer de enlace), streamline (optimizar, agilizar), feasible (viable, factible), leverage (aprovechar; influencia), diplomatic (diplomático), assertive (asertivo, firme), constructive (constructivo), convey (transmitir, comunicar), ascertain (averiguar, determinar), commitment (compromiso), on behalf of (en nombre de), acknowledge (reconocer; acusar recibo).

Lectura: The feedback sandwich is stale · Writing: Write an email to a senior colleague disagreeing diplomatically with a decision and proposing an alternative (180–250 words).

✍️ Unidad 3: Advanced Reading & Writing

  • ✓ Read complex texts and understand implicit meaning
  • ✓ Use emphasis and cleft sentences
  • ✓ Write a formal argumentative essay

Vocabulario: nuance (matiz), ambiguous (ambiguo), compelling (convincente, irresistible), concise (conciso), coherent (coherente), depict (representar, describir), vivid (vívido), subtle (sutil), irony (ironía), elaborate (desarrollar, explicar en detalle), draft (borrador), paraphrase (parafrasear).

Lectura: In praise of slow reading · Writing: Write a formal argumentative essay: "Schools should prioritise critical thinking over memorisation." (250–300 words)
