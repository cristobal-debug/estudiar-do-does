Al terminar el nivel B2 podrás…

  • ✓ Hold fluent conversations with native speakers
  • ✓ Write clear professional emails and essays
  • ✓ Understand news, arguments and complex texts
  • ✓ Speculate and argue about hypothetical situations

Unidades

💬 Unidad 1: Advanced Conversation

  • ✓ Keep a conversation going naturally
  • ✓ Speculate about the past (must have, might have)
  • ✓ Express regret and criticism (should have)

Vocabulario: small talk (charla trivial), awkward (incómodo, violento), gossip (cotilleo; cotillear), turn up (aparecer, presentarse), catch up (ponerse al día), get on with (llevarse bien con), bring up (sacar (un tema); criar), misunderstanding (malentendido), tactful (con tacto, diplomático), reliable (fiable, de confianza), hint (indirecta, pista), regret (arrepentirse; arrepentimiento).

Lectura: The art of small talk · Writing: Write about a decision you regret and what you should have done (100–150 words).

📊 Unidad 2: Business English

  • ✓ Write clear professional emails
  • ✓ Take part in meetings
  • ✓ Use impersonal passive structures

Vocabulario: budget (presupuesto), figures (cifras), agenda (orden del día), launch (lanzar; lanzamiento), supplier (proveedor), invoice (factura), stakeholder (parte interesada), revenue (ingresos), negotiate (negociar), postpone (aplazar), follow up (hacer seguimiento), deal (acuerdo, trato).

Lectura: Meetings that actually work · Writing: Write a formal email to a client announcing a delay and proposing a solution (100–150 words).

📰 Unidad 3: News & Media

  • ✓ Understand news stories
  • ✓ Report what people said
  • ✓ Tell facts from opinions

Vocabulario: headline (titular), journalist (periodista), source (fuente), claim (afirmar; afirmación), bias (sesgo, parcialidad), coverage (cobertura (informativa)), misleading (engañoso), deny (negar), announce (anunciar), breaking news (noticia de última hora), election (elecciones), eventually (finalmente, al final).

Lectura: Can we trust what we read? · Writing: Report a conversation or interview you had recently, using reported speech (100–150 words).

⚖️ Unidad 4: Debate & Arguments

  • ✓ Build a structured argument
  • ✓ Respond to opposing views politely
  • ✓ Talk about past hypotheticals (third & mixed conditionals)

Vocabulario: argument (argumento; discusión), evidence (pruebas, evidencia), persuade (convencer, persuadir), justify (justificar), assumption (suposición), drawback (inconveniente, desventaja), benefit (beneficio, ventaja), outweigh (superar, pesar más que), controversial (polémico), compromise (término medio; ceder), remote work (teletrabajo), consequence (consecuencia).

Lectura: Remote work: the debate continues · Writing: Write an opinion essay: "Remote work does more good than harm." (150–200 words)

🧩 Unidad 5: Complex Grammar

  • ✓ Use inversion for emphasis
  • ✓ Combine ideas in complex sentences
  • ✓ Understand formal written English

Vocabulario: findings (resultados, hallazgos), significant (significativo, importante), hypothesis (hipótesis), species (especie), climate change (cambio climático), emissions (emisiones), renewable (renovable), experiment (experimento), data (datos), trend (tendencia), impact (impacto), sustainable (sostenible).

Lectura: Why we sleep · Writing: Summarise a scientific or environmental issue you care about, using complex sentences (150–200 words).

Gramática B2

Modal perfectsModal + have + participleSpeculate and judge the past: must have, might have, can't have, should have.✗ He must be at home yesterday. → ✓ He must have been at home yesterday.
Advanced passiveImpersonal passive & causativeIt is said that…, He is believed to…, I had my car repaired.
Reported speechIndirect speechShe said (that) she would come. He asked me if I was ready.✗ He said me the truth. → ✓ He told me the truth.
Third and mixed conditionalsPast hypotheticalsIf I had known, I would have come. If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.
Inversion for emphasisNegative adverbialsNever have I seen… Not only did she… Rarely do we…
Complex sentencesParticiple clauses & linkingHaving finished…, Although…, Despite…, which… — combine ideas elegantly.
