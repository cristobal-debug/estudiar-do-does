Inglés B2

  1. 01

    Advanced Conversation Conversación avanzada

    Business English Inglés de negocios

Gramática del B2

Modal perfects
Deducir y juzgar el pasado: must have, might have, can't have, should have.He must be at home yesterday. → He must have been at home yesterday.
Advanced passive
It is said that…, He is believed to… y lo que encargas a otros: I had my car repaired.
Reported speech
Contar lo que otra persona dijo o preguntó: She said she would come. He asked me if I was ready.He said me the truth. → He told me the truth.
Third and mixed conditionals
Lo que pudo haber pasado y no pasó: If I had known, I would have come.
Inversion for emphasis
Énfasis formal con adverbios negativos al principio: Never have I seen… Not only did she…
Complex sentences
Unir varias ideas en una frase: Having finished…, Although…, Despite…, which…
