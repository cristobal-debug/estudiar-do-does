Gramática del B1

Present Perfect
Experiencias, noticias recientes y situaciones que siguen hasta hoy (for / since).I live here since three years. → I've lived here for three years.
Past Continuous
El fondo de una historia: I was walking home when it started to rain.When I arrived, she cooked. → When I arrived, she was cooking.
Gerunds and infinitives
Enjoy doing, want to do, good at doing… y to + verbo para decir «para».I study English for travel. → I study English to travel.
Relative clauses
Who para personas, which para cosas, where para lugares, whose para posesión.The girl which I met. → The girl (who / that) I met.
Passive voice
It was built in 1889. English is spoken here. Importa la acción, no quién la hace.Is spoken English. / Speaks English. → English is spoken.
First Conditional
Posibilidades reales en el futuro: If it rains, we'll stay at home.If it will rain, we stay. → If it rains, we'll stay.
Second Conditional
Situaciones imaginarias: If I won the lottery, I would travel.If I would have money… → If I had money…
