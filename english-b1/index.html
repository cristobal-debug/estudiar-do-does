Unidades

🌍 Unidad 1: Experiences · Experiencias

  • ✓ Talk about life experiences (Have you ever…?)
  • ✓ Tell a story with background actions
  • ✓ Choose between past simple and present perfect

Vocabulario: experience (experiencia), adventure (aventura), unforgettable (inolvidable), embarrassed (avergonzado (¡no embarazada!)), relieved (aliviado), scary (que da miedo), challenge (reto, desafío), memory (recuerdo; memoria), realise (darse cuenta), happen (pasar, ocurrir), suddenly (de repente), ever (alguna vez).

Lectura: The day I lost my passport · Writing: Describe an unforgettable experience (80–120 words).

💼 Unidad 2: Work & Career · Trabajo y carrera

  • ✓ Describe your job and responsibilities
  • ✓ Take part in a simple job interview
  • ✓ Use gerunds, infinitives and relative clauses

Vocabulario: career (carrera profesional), salary (sueldo, salario), colleague (compañero de trabajo), manager (jefe, gerente), interview (entrevista), apply for (solicitar (un puesto)), skills (habilidades, competencias), deadline (fecha límite, plazo), hire (contratar), resign (dimitir, renunciar), promotion (ascenso), part-time (a tiempo parcial).

Lectura: Working from anywhere · Writing: Write a short email applying for a job you would like.

🏛️ Unidad 3: Travel & Culture · Viajes y cultura

  • ✓ Describe traditions and famous places
  • ✓ Use the passive to talk about history and processes
  • ✓ Compare customs in different countries

Vocabulario: custom (costumbre), tradition (tradición), festival (festival, fiesta), heritage (patrimonio), century (siglo), local (local, de la zona), monument (monumento), ancient (antiguo (muy antiguo)), crowded (abarrotado, lleno de gente), worth (que merece la pena), rude (maleducado, grosero), tip (propina; consejo).

Lectura: Tipping around the world · Writing: Describe a famous place or tradition from your country (80–120 words).

📱 Unidad 4: Technology · Tecnología

  • ✓ Talk about gadgets, apps and the internet
  • ✓ Describe problems and solutions (If…, will…)
  • ✓ Give instructions for using technology

Vocabulario: device (dispositivo), app (aplicación), download (descargar), update (actualizar; actualización), password (contraseña), battery (batería), screen (pantalla), log in (iniciar sesión), settings (ajustes, configuración), charge (cargar (batería)), research (investigación; investigar), online (en línea, por internet).

Lectura: Digital detox · Writing: Write a short guide: "5 tips to use your phone less".

🗣️ Unidad 5: Opinions & Society · Opiniones y sociedad

  • ✓ Give and justify opinions
  • ✓ Agree and disagree politely
  • ✓ Talk about hypothetical situations (If I were…)

Vocabulario: opinion (opinión), agree (estar de acuerdo), disagree (no estar de acuerdo), argue (discutir; argumentar), society (sociedad), government (gobierno), law (ley), vote (votar; voto), fair (justo), pollution (contaminación), recycle (reciclar), community (comunidad).

Lectura: Should homework be banned? · Writing: Opinion paragraph: Should public transport be free?

Gramática B1

Present PerfectPretérito perfecto (have + participio)Experiences, recent news and situations that continue until now (for / since).✗ I live here since three years. → ✓ I've lived here for three years.
Past ContinuousPasado continuo (was / were + -ing)Background actions in stories: I was walking home when it started to rain.✗ When I arrived, she cooked. → ✓ When I arrived, she was cooking.
Gerunds and infinitivesGerundio (-ing) o infinitivo (to)Enjoy doing, want to do, good at doing, to + verb for purpose.✗ I study English for travel. → ✓ I study English to travel.
Relative clausesOraciones de relativo (who, which, that, where)Who for people, which for things, where for places, whose for possession.✗ The girl which I met. → ✓ The girl (who / that) I met.
Passive voiceLa voz pasiva (be + participio)It was built in 1889. English is spoken here. Focus on the action, not the agent.✗ Is spoken English. / Speaks English. → ✓ English is spoken.
First ConditionalPrimer condicional (If + presente, will)Real possibilities in the future: If it rains, we'll stay at home.✗ If it will rain, we stay. → ✓ If it rains, we'll stay.
Second ConditionalSegundo condicional (If + pasado, would)Imaginary situations: If I won the lottery, I would travel.✗ If I would have money… → ✓ If I had money…
