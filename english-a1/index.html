Gramática del A1

Verb to be
Am, is, are: el verbo más importante del inglés. Significa ser y estar.I have 20 years. → I am 20 years old.
Subject pronouns
I, you, he, she, it, we, they. En inglés el sujeto nunca se omite.Is very expensive. → It's very expensive.
Possessive adjectives
My, your, his, her, its, our, their… y el genitivo sajón (Maria's car).María and his brother. → María and her brother.
Plurals
Regla general -s, casos -es / -ies e irregulares (men, children, people).Reds cars. → Red cars.
Present Simple
Rutinas y verdades generales. He / she / it llevan -s; negativas y preguntas con do / does.What you want? → What do you want?
Do or make?
Dos verbos para "hacer": make para crear, do para actividades.I did a mistake. → I made a mistake.
There is / There are
Para decir "hay": there is + singular, there are + plural.In my city has a museum. → In my city there is a museum.
Articles: a, an, the
A / an para algo nuevo o una profesión; the para algo concreto; nada para hablar en general.I am teacher. → I am a teacher.
Prepositions of time and place
At + hora, on + día, in + mes. Next to, opposite, between…In Monday. → On Monday.
Can / Can't
Habilidad, posibilidad y permiso. Nunca cambia y va sin to.I know swim. / I can to swim. → I can swim.
Capital letters & full stops
Idiomas, nacionalidades, días, meses, siglas y el pronombre I van con mayúscula.i speak spanish on monday. → I speak Spanish on Monday.
